About us

At Bakri Design Studio, we turn an idea into a real space that reflects its owner. We work on interior and exterior projects of every kind, from homes to shops, offices and company headquarters.

We care about the details: materials, lighting, space planning and color, so every project is both practical and luxurious.

Our services

We cover the project types a space owner needs, from the first idea to the final look.

  • Residential design

    Apartments and homes designed for comfort and luxury, shaped around how you live and using every meter wisely.

  • Commercial design

    Commercial spaces that express your brand and attract customers at first glance.

  • Administrative design

    Elegant, practical offices and work environments that support your team and reflect your organization.

  • Business spaces

    Company headquarters, meeting rooms and reception areas with a professional character that leaves a strong impression.

  • Exterior design

    Facades and outdoor spaces that give your project a clear presence and complete its interior design.
